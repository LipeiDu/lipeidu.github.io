## University courses

### Majors Electricity and Magnetism (PHYS 340)

**McGill University · Fall 2022 · Instructor of record**

Taught a three-credit undergraduate physics-majors course (39 lecture hours) for approximately 50 students. Responsibilities included lectures, course notes, assignments, quizzes, examinations, and detailed solutions.

**Lecture notes:** [Electricity](https://github.com/LipeiDu/PHYS340-Electricity-and-Magnetism/blob/main/Lecture%20-%20electricity.pdf) · [Magnetism](https://github.com/LipeiDu/PHYS340-Electricity-and-Magnetism/blob/main/Lecture%20-%20magnetism.pdf)

### Introductory physics recitations and laboratories

**The Ohio State University · 2016–2017 · Teaching assistant**

Led recitations and supervised laboratory activities for calculus-based introductory classical physics in Fall 2016 and Spring 2017. Graded assessments and laboratory reports and supported students in small groups.

### Mathematical and theoretical physics courses

**Peking University · 2012–2013 · Teaching assistant**

Supported instruction in Methods of Mathematical Physics (Fall 2012), Classical Electrodynamics (Spring 2013), and Quantum Mechanics (Fall 2013), conducting recitations, grading assessments, and providing individual support.

## Graduate lectures and summer schools

### Introduction to heavy-ion physics

**McGill University · Summer 2022 · Lecturer**

Designed and delivered a ten-lecture series (15 hours) for first-year graduate students in the Nuclear Theory Group, covering the foundations of heavy-ion collisions, multistage theoretical modeling, and relativistic hydrodynamics.

### JETSCAPE Online Summer School — Bulk dynamics

**JETSCAPE Collaboration · July 25–August 5, 2022 · Lecturer and hands-on instructor**

Taught hydrodynamics and bulk QGP dynamics to an international audience of graduate students and postdoctoral researchers from theory and experiment. Prepared and adapted computational exercises for exploring heavy-ion simulations within the JETSCAPE framework. Also served as a teaching assistant and secondary instructor at the 2020 and 2021 summer schools.

**Materials:** [Lecture slides](https://indico.global/event/1294/contributions/27108/attachments/13674/20775/Hydro%20-%20Lecture%20-%20L%20Du.pdf) · [Computational exercises and notebooks](https://github.com/JETSCAPE/SummerSchool2022/tree/main/Jul28_Hydro) · [Summer school program and recordings](https://indico.global/event/1294/timetable/)
